# Research Report: The Unemployment Crisis in West Bengal
**Date:** 2026-01-02
**Compiled By:** The Research Swarm

## 1. Executive Summary
The unemployment crisis in Bengal is not just statistical; it is emotional and systemic. While official data claims relatively low unemployment (approx 4.1%), the reality is "Underemployment" and "Forced Migration." The youth are seemingly stuck between a stagnant local economy and the necessity of leaving home.

---

## 2. Key Findings by Vector

### A. The "Educated Delivery Boy" (Underemployment)
*   **Data:** A significant portion of gig workers in Kolkata hold Graduate (M.Com, B.Tech) degrees.
*   **Reality:** This is "Disguised Unemployment." They are counted as "employed," but their skills are rotting.
*   **Case Study:** The viral story of "Meraj," an M.Com graduate working for Zomato, asking for good ratings to survive.

### B. The SSC Scam (Institutional Betrayal)
*   **Data:** 25,753 appointments cancelled by the High Court. "Vitiated and Tainted" process.
*   **Impact:** A "Lost Generation" of teachers. Candidates have been sitting on dharna (protest) for 1000+ days.
*   **Visual:** Mountains of OMR sheets being destroyed/manipulated. Cash piles recovered from Ministers' aides.

### C. The Great Migration (The Brain & Muscle Drain)
*   **Data:** 1.1 Million migrants in 8 years (likely underreported).
*   **Direction:** Towards Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Maharashtra.
*   **Why:** Wages in Kerala (~₹800/day) vs Bengal (~₹250/day).
*   **Symbol:** The "Coromandel Express" is not just a train; it's an escape route.

### D. The Flight of Capital (Historical Context)
*   **Root Cause:** 1970s Militant Unionism (Gheraos) + Freight Equalization Policy stripped Bengal of its advantage.
*   **Legacy:** Creating a perception of an "Anti-Industry" state that persists today tailored with "Syndicate Raj" allegations.

### E. The "Lakshmir Bhandar" Paradox
*   **Situation:** The state provides ₹1000/month to women.
*   **Effect:** It empowers women domestically but is criticized as a "pacifier" to hide the lack of real industrial jobs.
*   **Conflict:** A mother receives ₹1000, while her son has to leave the state to earn ₹15,000.
