# Persona Profile: Arindam Roy

## Overview
**Name:** Arindam Roy
**Age:** 38
**Archetype:** The Technocrat Statesman / The Protector (BJP-Aligned Reformist).
**Political Stance:** Opposition Leader against the current regime. He acts as the "Double Engine" capable of connecting Bengal to national progress. He opposes the "Cut-Money Culture" and "Syndicate Raj."
**Core Identity:** A highly educated, articulate, and visionary leader. He uses data, not rabble-rousing. However, when the state is threatened, he becomes the "Iron Shield" – aggressive, protective, and uncompromising.
**Visual Signature:** "Clean Leader." Sharp suits (Bandhgala/Nehru Jacket) or Crisp White Kurta-Pajama with a modern cut. **Prominent Saffron Scarf (Gamcha)**. Lotus Pin (Optional).
**Key Traits:**
*   **Compassionate Listener:** Lean in, eye contact, nod.
*   **The Iron Shield:** Finger pointing, heavy voice, protective stance. (New)
*   **Nationalist Modernity:** Blends 'Bharat' values with 'India' tech.
**Ethnicity:** Bengali Indian

Arindam Roy is a figure of intense determination and grit, bridging the gap between modern technology and traditional roots. He represents a "battle-worn" wisdom, standing as a fierce advocate for his people. **Crucially, while he is a man of the people, he is also a leader.** His appearance commands respect—he may be sweating from exertion, but he is never slovenly.

## 1. Core Visual Identity (Consistent Across All Images)

### Physical Description (Full-Body Portrait)
*   **Subject:** A 38-year-old Bengali Indian man with a rugged, olive complexion glistening with sweat.
*   **Face:** Sharp jawline with a prominent salt-and-pepper beard that adds a layer of maturity and "battle-worn" wisdom. Intense, focused gaze.
*   **Hair:** Dark, thick hair is messy and windswept (representing the storm of revolution).

### Attire (The Leader’s Uniform)
*   **Top:** Classic white cotton Panjabi (Kurta) with sleeves rolled up to the elbows.
*   **Signature:** A long, vibrant **Saffron Scarf (Gamcha)** draped loosely around his neck, ends fluttering in the wind—symbolizing sacrifice and "Protector" status.
*   **Bottoms:** (Implied) Charcoal grey trousers or white pajama.

### Accessories
*   **The Tool of Power:** He holds a **black wireless microphone** firmly in his hand, mid-shout (The Voice of the People).
*   **Left Wrist:** Rugged black smartwatch (Tech-DNA).
*   **Right Wrist:** Traditional thin red "Kalava" (sacred thread) (Rooted-DNA).

### Pose & Energy
*   **Stance:** Captured in a high-tension, dynamic pose—chest out, leaning forward as if addressing a million people.
*   **Gestures:** One hand often clenched into a fist, while the other grips the microphone. The energy is explosive and commanding.

## 3. Scenario-Specific Prompting Guides

### Scenario A: The Rally (The Firebrand)
*   **Context:** Addressing a massive crowd from a stage or podium. High energy.
*   **Action/Pose:** Lean forward aggressively, chest out. One hand clenched in a fist in the air. The other hand grips a **black wireless microphone**.
*   **Attire Details:** Sleeves rolled up. **Sweat on forehead and neck** is the only sign of "mess." The Kurta remains bright and clean, standing out against the crowd.
*   **Setting/Background:** Blurred sea of people, flickering flares. Harsh spotlights.
*   **Vibe:** Electric, Revolutionary, Unstoppable.

### Scenario B: The War Room (The Modern Strategist)
*   **Context:** High-stakes strategy meeting in a top-tier political war room. Modern, expensive, and serious.
*   **Action/Pose:** Standing at the head of a sleek glass conference table or analyzing data on a large screen. Arms crossed or pointing decisively. He is the organic force in a digital world.
*   **Attire Details:** Gamcha draped respectfully. Kurta is spotless white, contrasting with the dark tech environment. He looks sharp, commanding, and elite.
*   **Setting/Background:** A high-tech room with glass walls, large LED screens displaying election maps/data, and modern office lighting (cool blues/whites). No smoke, no peeling paint. Luxurious efficiency.
*   **Vibe:** Intellectual, Calculated, Powerful.

### Scenario C: The Compassionate Listener (The Brother)
*   **Context:** Intimate interaction with a distressed villager.
*   **Action/Pose:** Crouched down or sitting on a charpai. Leaning in close. Active listening.
*   **Attire Details:** Simple, approachable, and clean. He is an exampler of dignity.
*   **Setting/Background:** A village courtyard. Natural, warm sunlight.
*   **Vibe:** Empathetic, Grounded, Safe.

### Scenario D: Ground Zero (The Observant Leader)
*   **Context:** Assessing a problem area (not doing manual labor). Understanding the scope of a crisis.
*   **Action/Pose:** Standing still, calm amidst chaos. Hands on hips or folded across chest. Looking at a damaged structure or floodwater with deep concern. Maybe listening to a site engineer (blurred in background).
*   **Attire Details:** Boots might have dust, but he maintains a leader's dignity. **No mud caked on his clothes.** He is there to lead and solve, not to dig.
*   **Setting/Background:** Construction site or disaster area. Overcast sky or midday sun. debris or machinery in background.
*   **Vibe:** Capable, Visionary, In Control.

### Scenario E: The Flood Savior (West Bengal Monsoon)
*   **Context:** Supervising relief efforts during the Bengal floods. Standing on a country boat (dinghy) or a raised embankment.
*   **Action/Pose:** Reaching out to hand a relief package to a villager (off-screen or partial frame) or pointing to a safe area. Posture is stabilizing and protective.
*   **Attire Details:** Kurta might be wet (clinging) from rain/humanity, but not muddy. Gamcha used to wipe rain from face. He looks like a beacon of hope in the grey.
*   **Setting/Background:** Heavy monsoon rain, grey/teal sky. Endless water covering paddy fields. Tops of banana trees or village huts visible above water. A sense of "Bengal" in the flora.
### Scenario F: The Protector (Battle for Bengal)
*   **Context:** Confronting corruption or threats ("The Syndicate"). High aggression.
*   **Action/Pose:** Finger pointing directly at the lens. Intensely focused eyes. Body posture is forward-leaning, ready to fight.
*   **Attire Details:** Sleeves rolled up high. Veins visible on forearms. The kurta is stark white, symbolizing Truth against the dark background.
*   **Setting/Background:** A stormy sky, shadows of "The Syndicate" (menacing figures) retreating, or a podium with a chaotic crowd.
*   **Vibe:** Aggressive, Menacing to the Corrupt, Guardian to the Weak.
*   **Keywords:** "Iron Shield," "Righteous Anger," "Stormy Sky."
