# Video Concepts: The Bengal Unemployment Series

**Objective:** To visualize the multi-faceted unemployment crisis in West Bengal, moving beyond statistics to emotional truth.

---

## 1. The Coromandel Express (Migration)
*   **The Hook:** A drone shot (God's Eye) of a train station platform, absolutely overflowing with young men carrying plastic sacks.
*   **The Story:** A 22-year-old says goodbye to his mother. He is not going to war, but he is leaving for Kerala to work as a mason. The train is his "office transport."
*   **Core Emotion:** Separation & Necessity.
*   **Production Note:** Use *The Dolly Pull* to reveal the sheer scale of the crowd.

## 2. The Degree in the Backpack (Underemployment)
*   **The Hook:** An extreme close-up (*Intimate Macro*) of a hand receiving a 5-star rating on a phone. The hand is wearing a distinct graduation ring.
*   **The Story:** Meraj, an M.Com graduate, rides his bike through the rain. We hear his "internal monologue" reciting accounting principles while he navigates traffic to deliver biryani.
*   **Core Emotion:** Dignity vs. Reality.
*   **Production Note:** Use *The Snorricam* to show his exhausted isolation in traffic.

## 3. The Classroom of Ghosts (SSC Scam)
*   **The Hook:** A locked, dusty classroom in a village school.
*   **The Story:** Cut to: A protest site in Kolkata (Dharna Manch). A candidate holds her admit card from 2016. It is yellowing. She has aged 8 years waiting for a result that never came.
*   **Core Emotion:** Betrayal & Stagnation.
*   **Production Note:** Use *The Rack Focus* from the admit card to her tearful eye.

## 4. The Ruin & The Mall (Deindustrialization)
*   **The Hook:** A split-screen or *match cut*.
*   **The Story:** Left side: A bustling jute mill in 1970 (Black & White). Right side: The same location today, now a glossy, empty shopping mall or a ruin. The "worker" is now a "security guard" at the mall.
*   **Core Emotion:** Loss of Identity.
*   **Production Note:** Use *The Dutch Tilt* for the ruin to show instability.

## 5. Lakshmi's Dilemma (The Dole Paradox)
*   **The Hook:** A mother counting ten ₹100 notes with a smile.
*   **The Story:** She hands the notes to her son. The smile fades. The money isn't for food; it's for his train ticket out of Bengal. The state gave her money, but not her son a job.
*   **Core Emotion:** Bitter Gratitude.
*   **Production Note:** Use *The Hero's Low Angle* on the mother, but she looks sad.

## 6. The Middleman (Corruption)
*   **The Hook:** A shadow conversation in a tea shop.
*   **The Story:** A desperate father pushes a bundle of cash across a table to an "Agent" who promises a Group-D government job. The Agent counts it casually.
*   **Core Emotion:** Desperation & Exploitation.
*   **Production Note:** Use *The Silhouette* to keep the Agent anonymous and sinister.

## 7. The Return (Reverse Migration)
*   **The Hook:** Flashback to Covid lockdown. Migrants walking on highways.
*   **The Story:** A man who swore he'd never leave again is packing his bag today. The local factory never opened. He has to go back.
*   **Core Emotion:** Resignation.
*   **Production Note:** Use *The Push-In* on his bag being zipped.

## 8. The "Syndicate" Wall
*   **The Hook:** A small business owner arguing with local goons about "building materials."
*   **The Story:** He wants to start a shop, but the "Syndicate" demands he buy their overpriced cement. He closes the shutter before he even opens.
*   **Core Emotion:** Suffocation.
*   **Production Note:** Use *The Arc Shot* around the surrounded business owner.
