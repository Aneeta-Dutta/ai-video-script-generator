# The Costume Designer's Bible: Textile Physics & Breakdown (Masterclass Edition)

## 1. The "Clean Leader" Doctrine (Updated Protocol)
**Code:** `LEADER_CLEAN_V2`
**Principles:**
*   **The Exampler:** Clothes are a symbol of *Dharma*. They must appear maintained.
*   **Labor vs. Neglect:** Sweat is labor (Honorable). Dirt is neglect (Disrespectful).
*   **The "Crisp" Factor:** Even when wet, the structural integrity of the garment remains. It does not become a rag.

## 2. Garment Specifications

### A. The Panjabi (Kurta) - Technical Specs
*   **Fabric:** 100% Handloom Khadi Cotton.
*   **Weight:** 80-100 GSM (Lightweight, breathable).
*   **Weave:** Plain Weave with slight *Slub Yarn* irregularities. This catches the light (Rim Light).
*   **Drape Physics:** Soft body cloth. It follows the contours of the body but maintains a semi-rigid silhouette at the shoulders.
*   **Aging/Breakdown:**
    *   *Wash:* Stone-washed for softness (Micro-abrasion).
    *   *Wrinkle Map:* "Stress Wrinkles" at elbows and waist (Accordion folds). NO random crinkles.

### B. The Gamcha (Scarf) - Specifics
*   **Fabric:** Coarse Cotton Mesh.
*   **Origin:** Bankura or Murshidabad weave.
*   **Color:** Saffron (Pantone 158 C).
*   **Physics:** High wind resistance. It should "flag" in the wind, rigid enough to hold shape, not silky or drooping.
*   **Knot:** Draped loosely (The Loop) or tied efficiently. Never styled like a fashion accessory.

### C. The Footwear - The Anchor
*   **Model:** Service Boot / Chukka hybrid.
*   **Material:** Full-grain leather, matte finish (Nubuck).
*   **Patina:** "Road Dust" glaze on the toe box. Sole edges should show wear.
*   **Lacing:** Practical, tight lacing. No loose ends.

## 3. Accessory Details (Prop Master)
*   **Wrist Tech:** *Rugged Black Smartwatch* (e.g., Apple Watch Ultra with matte casing). Screen OFF or displaying simple data (Heart Rate).
*   **Sacred Thread (Kalava):** Traditional Thin Red Kalava. Frayed ends (indicating age).
*   **Microphone:** *Black Wireless Microphone* (Sennheiser SKM 6000 or Shure SM58). Matte black grille.

## 4. Textile Rendering Directives
*   **Sub-Surface Scattering (SSS):** Enable on the Kurta to show light passing through the thin cotton (backlit scenarios).
*   **Translucency:** High. Sweat patches should turn the white fabric slightly transparent/grey.
*   **Specular:** Low. Fabric is matte. Only sweat has specular value.

## 5. How to Use This Guide in Prompting
**Core Syntax:**
> "Dress Arindam in [Garment Item]. Ensure fabric shows [Texture/Weave details]. Add [Accessory] to symbolize [Meaning]."

**Examples:**
*   "Dress Arindam in his **Khadi Panjabi**. Ensure fabric shows **stone-washed texture** and **stress wrinkles**. Add a **Saffron Gamcha** for wind resistance."
*   "Focus on his feet. Show **Service Boots** with **Road Dust patina**."
