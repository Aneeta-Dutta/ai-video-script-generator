# The Orator's Lexicon: Dialogue & Sloganeering (Masterclass Edition)

## 1. The Linguistic Philosophy: "The Roar and The Whisper"
**Core Directive:** Language is a tool of *Connection* and *Combat*.
*   **The Roar:** For the masses. High volume, rhythmic, hypnotic.
*   **The Whisper:** For the individual. Intimate, truthful, conspiratorial.

## 2. The 8-Second Canvas (Temporal Constraints)
A standard Veo 3 generation is 8 seconds. Dialogue must fit this window perfectly.
*   **Word Count Limit:** 15-20 words (Maximum).
*   **Pacing:**
    *   * Seconds 0-2:* The Hook (Statement of Fact).
    *   * Seconds 3-6:* The Emotion (The Twist/Expansion).
    *   * Seconds 7-8:* The Resolve (The Punchline).
*   **Example:** "They broke the bridge. (Hook) --> Now they want us to swim across the flood with joy? (Emotion) --> Madmen. (Resolve)"

## 3. Dialect Protocols (The Bengali Spectrum)

### A. High Bengali (Bhadralok Tone)
*   **Context:** Press Conferences, Manifestos, Solemn Pledges.
*   **Vocabulary:** Sanskritized words (*Odhikar*, *Oitijhyo*, *Poriborton*).
*   **Tone:** Educated, restrained, statesman-like.
*   **Example:** "Amra shudhu protishruti di na, amra *bastobayan* kori." (We don't just give promises, we implement.)

### B. Standard Colloquial (Cholitobhasha - The Clean Leader)
*   **Context:** Interviews, Youtube Videos, "Mann Ki Baat" style chats.
*   **Vocabulary:** Everyday words, clear pronunciation, no slang.
*   **Tone:** Warm, accessible, uncle-next-door.
*   **Example:** "Apnara shobai janen, kaj ta koto kothin." (You all know how hard the work is.)

### C. The Street Hook (Rokey / Kolkata Gritty)
*   **Context:** Confronting corruption, "The Syndicate," Punchlines.
*   **Vocabulary:** Sharp, cutting, slightly rough edges. Use of irony.
*   **Focus:** Punching up at authority.
*   **Example:** "Chor dhoro, Jail bhoro!" (Catch the thieves, fill the jails!)

## 3. The Science of the Slogan (Sloganeering)

### A. The Rhyme Scheme (AABB)
*   **Technique:** Simple, chantable rhymes.
*   **Structure:**
    *   Line 1: Setup the Problem (*Durniti r pahar* - Mountain of corruption)
    *   Line 2: Offer the Solution (*Arindam r prohar* - Arindam's strike)

### B. The Rhythmic Triad (Rule of Three)
*   **Technique:** Three distinct words that build momentum.
*   **Example:** "Chakri. Shiksha. Nirapatta." (Jobs. Education. Security.)

### C. The Irony Twist
*   **Technique:** Taking a government claim and twisting it.
*   **Example:** *Claim:* "Unnoyon" (Development). *Twist:* "Unnoyon naki Prohoson?" (Development or Farce?)

## 4. Contextual Dialogue Templates

### Scenario: The Tea Shop (Despair)
*   **Dialogue Style:** Fragmented sentences, rhetorical questions.
*   **Key Phrase:** "Ar koto din?" (How much longer?)
*   **Vibe:** Cynicism mixed with faint hope.

### Scenario: The Rally (Hope)
*   **Dialogue Style:** Call and Response.
*   **Key Phrase:** "Awaj tolo!" (Raise your voice!)
*   **Vibe:** Electric, unifying.

## 5. How to Use This Guide in Prompting
**Core Syntax:**
> "Write a [Type of Speech] for Arindam using [Dialect]. It should focus on [Topic] and end with a [Technique]."

**Examples:**
*   "Write a **Rally Slogan** for Arindam using **Street Hook**. It should focus on **Unemployment** and use a **Rhyme Scheme**."
*   "Write a **Tea Shop Whisper** using **Standard Colloquial**. Focus on the **SSC Scam** betrayal."
