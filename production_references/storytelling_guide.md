# The Storyteller's Guide: Hollywood Transitions & Cinematic Glue (Masterclass Edition)

**Objective:** To move beyond list-making and master *Sequence Construction*.
**Core Problem:** A list of scenes is not a story. A story is the *friction* between scenes.

---

## 1. The Physics of Transition (The "Handshake")
You cannot just jump from A to B. You must build a bridge.

### A. The Visual Match Cut (The Shape Bridge)
*   **Concept:** The end frame of Scene A matches the geometric shape of the start frame of Scene B.
*   **Prompting Logic:**
    *   *Scene A End:* "...camera zooms into the round graduation ring."
    *   *Scene B Start:* "...camera zooms out from a round bike wheel."
*   **Effect:** Subconscious continuity. The viewer feels the connection before they understand it.

### B. The Audio Bridge (J-Cut / L-Cut)
*   **Concept:** The audio from one scene bleeds into another.
*   **The L-Cut (The Haunting):** The dialogue from Scene A continues over the visuals of Scene B.
    *   *Example:* Mother says "Don't go..." (Scene 1). We *see* the Train leaving (Scene 2), but we still *hear* her echo "...go."
*   **The J-Cut (The Premonition):** The sound of Scene B starts while we are still watching Scene A.
    *   *Example:* We see the Student staring at his degree (Scene 1). We hear the *revving of a bike engine* (Scene 2) *before* we cut to the bike.

### C. The Kinetic Wipe (The Motion Bridge)
*   **Concept:** Momentum carries through the cut.
*   **Logic:** If Scene A ends with a pan *Right*, Scene B MUST start with a pan *Right* or a movement *Entering from Left*.

---

## 2. Narrative Velocity (Pacing)
*   **The Breath:** Open with a static, wide shot (The context).
*   **The Tension:** Move to moving, tracking shots (The conflict).
*   **The Impact:** End with a static, macro close-up (The consequence).

---

## 3. How to Write a Sequence (The Template)

**Don't write:**
> Scene 1: A boy leaves home.
> Scene 2: He gets on a train.

**Write:**
> **Scene 1:** The boy touches his mother's feet. **Cut to:** The mother looking down.
> **Transition Hook:** The sound of a *train whistle* screams over the mother's face.
> **Scene 2:** Smash cut to the Train Wheels screeching (matching the whistle scream).

---

## 4. The Editor's Checklist
Before approving a sequence, ask:
1.  **Where is the Glue?** Is there a visual or audio link?
2.  **Is the Geography clear?** Did we break the 180-degree rule?
3.  **Is the Emotion continuous?** Does the sadness of Scene A pay off in Scene B?
