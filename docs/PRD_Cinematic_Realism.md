# PRD: Cinematic Realism & Multi-Agent Orchestration

## 1. Product Vision
To build an AI-driven production house that converts abstract research into professional-grade, 8-second cinematic "bytes" with absolute continuity in character, performance, and visual fidelity.

## 2. Target Audience
- **Film Houses:** Pre-visualization of high-budget sequences.
- **Digital Newsrooms:** Transforming investigative reports into realistic visual narratives.
- **Indie Creators:** Achieving "Hollywood-level" visuals with localized (Bengali) authenticity.

## 3. Key Requirements

### 3.1. Character Continuity (The "Persona Anchor")
- **RP1:** Every character MUST have a unique "Vocal DNA" and "Physicality" defined at the project start.
- **RP2:** The system must use a persistent Persona DB to inject these anchors into every agent's context.

### 3.2. Performance Authenticity (The "Kobiyal Protocol")
- **RP3:** Dialogue must be in native West Bengal Standard Bengali.
- **RP4:** Lip-sync intensity and phonetic markers must be provided for downstream audio-visual synchronization.
- **RP5:** Performance notes must guide the emotive delivery (e.g., "suppressed rage").

### 3.3. Visual Fidelity (The "Nolan Protocol")
- **RP6:** Visuals must avoid generic AI looks by using PBR (Physically Based Rendering) and ray-tracing physics in prompts.
- **RP7:** Camera specs must mimic real gear (65mm IMAX, 50mm Prime, Panavision).
- **RP8:** Lighting must follow Tyndall effect and Sub-surface scattering rules for realistic skin.

### 3.4. Orchestration
- **RP9:** Multi-scene "beats" (Intro, Build, Climax, Outro) must be maintained.
- **RP10:** State tracking must carry over character changes (e.g., "Sweat-drenched" or "Tattered clothes") across scenes.

## 4. Success Metrics
- **Substantive Volume:** >50k tokens per production session.
- **Coherence:** Visual consistency across 10 scenes > 90%.
- **Cultural Accuracy:** Zero usage of non-standard Bengali dialects in "Sophisticated Urban" contexts.

## 5. Technical Constraints
- Optimized for **Google Veo 3** ingestion.
- Powered by **Gemini 2.0 Flash**.
- Strictly sequential agent delegation to maximize context carry-over.
