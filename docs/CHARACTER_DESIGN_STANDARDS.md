# Character Design Standards: The "Persona Anchor" Protocol

## 1. Physicality Anchor
Every character generated by the **Executive Producer** must have a high-density physical description.
- **Mandatory Details:** Age, facial structure, skin texture (pores, scars, sub-surface scattering), hair maturity, and clothing fabric (material density, weathering).
- **Pro-Tip:** Instead of "wearing a kurta", use "wearing a hand-loomed, rumpled khadi kurta with subtle sweat stains around the collar".

## 2. Vocal DNA
The **Writer (Kobiyal)** and **Audio Post-house** need precise markers.
- **Accent:** Strictly "West Bengal Standard" or "North Kolkata" for urban settings. 
- **Timbre:** Specify if the voice is "deep baritone", "nasal", "raspy", or "silky".
- **Cadence:** Is it a fast-talking detective or a slow-measured scholar?

## 3. Emotional Performance Notes
Instructions for the actor's delivery.
- **Nuance:** Use micro-expression directives like "suppressed rage", "flicker of doubt in the eyes", or "jaded weariness".
- **Intensity:** Intro scenes should aim for 0.4-0.6, while climax beats demand 0.9-1.0 intensity.

## 4. State Continuity
Agents must track the **physical progression** of a character.
- **Scene 1:** Clean, composed.
- **Scene 5:** Sweat-drenched, heavy breathing.
- **Scene 10:** Exhausted, silhouette in the mist.
