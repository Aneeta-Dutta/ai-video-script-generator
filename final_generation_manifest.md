## Sequence 1: The Exodus (The Migrant's Arc)
**Transitions:** The Sound of the Train (Whistle/Wheels) binds the Mother's silence to the Son's departure.

### Scene 1: The Price of Separation
*   **Visual Hook (Start):** Fade in from Black.
*   **Audio Bridge (J-Cut):** (At 00:06) The faint, high-pitched scream of a train whistle starts *over* the mother's face.
*   **Veo 3 Prompt:**
    ```text
    [SUBJECT]: Low Angle shot of Rural Mother + Counting crumpled notes + Handing them to Son (Off-screen hand) :: [ENVIRONMENT]: Mud House Verandah :: [LIGHTING]: Chiaroscuro (Single bulb) :: [CAMERA]: Hero's Low Angle + 50mm Prime :: [POST]: Warm Tungsten balance --style cinematic
    ```
*   **Dialogue (Bengali):** "সরকার দিল হাতখরচ। তুই জানলি না, ওটা তোর বিচ্ছেদের দাম। যা, ঘুরে দেখ তোর ভাগ্য।"
*   **Translation:** "The government gave pocket money. You didn't know, that was the price of your separation. Go, find your fate."
*   **Visual Hook (End):** She closes her eyes. Screen fades to Match Cut on the son closing *his* eyes.

### Scene 2: The Vessel
*   **Visual Hook (Start):** Match Cut: Extreme Close Up of Son's eyes opening.
*   **Audio Bridge (L-Cut):** The train whistle from Scene 1 screams into full volume screeching wheels.
*   **Veo 3 Prompt:**
    ```text
    [SUBJECT]: Cinematic shot of Young Bengali Migrant + Clinging to train door handle + Train moving fast :: [ENVIRONMENT]: Coromandel Express + Steam and Dust :: [LIGHTING]: Sodium Vapor + Volumetric Fog :: [CAMERA]: God's Eye View pull-back :: [POST]: Teal & Orange --style cinematic
    ```
*   **Dialogue (Bengali):** "পিছনে ফেলে এলাম মাটির টান। সামনে শুধু অচেনা শহর আর ভাতের লড়াই।"
*   **Translation:** "I left behind the pull of the soil. Ahead is only unknown cities and the battle for rice."
*   **Visual Hook (End):** The train blurs into a horizontal motion line (Motion Blur).

### Scene 3: The Return
*   **Visual Hook (Start):** The horizontal motion blur slows down to become the *Zip of a Bag* moving horizontally.
*   **Audio Bridge (J-Cut):** The train sound dies down instantly to the sound of a zipper getting stuck.
*   **Veo 3 Prompt:**
    ```text
    [SUBJECT]: Close up of Hands zipping cheap travel bag + Camera moves up to Defeated Face :: [ENVIRONMENT]: Empty Room + Peeling blue paint :: [LIGHTING]: Window Light (Softbox) :: [CAMERA]: Snorricam Chest Mount (Shaky) :: [POST]: Desaturated Blue --style cinematic
    ```
*   **Dialogue (Bengali):** "ভেবেছিলাম পৃথিবীটা জিতব। শেষে, এই ভাঙা সুটকেসটাই আমার জয়ের ট্রফি। দেশ আমাকে রাখল না।"
*   **Translation:** "I thought I would conquer the world. In the end, this broken suitcase is my victory trophy. My country didn't keep me."

## Sequence 2: The Mirage (The Student's Arc)
**Transitions:** The Color Gold (Ring) transitions to the Red (Tail Light).

### Scene 1: The Promise
*   **Visual Hook (Start):** Static shot of a Gold Ring.
*   **Audio Bridge (J-Cut):** (At 00:06) The sound of heavy rain pouring starts.
*   **Veo 3 Prompt:**
    ```text
    [SUBJECT]: Macro Shot of Gold Graduation Ring (University Logo) on finger + Tapping on Smartphone Screen :: [ENVIRONMENT]: Rain-slicked asphalt background :: [LIGHTING]: Neon Reflections on wet metal :: [CAMERA]: Oppenheimer Macro Lens + Shallow Depth of Field :: [POST]: High Contrast --style cinematic
    ```
*   **Dialogue (Bengali):** "কলমের জোর ছিল, কিন্তু কপালে জোর ছিল না। এই আংটিটা এখন শুধুই একটা তামাশা।"
*   **Translation:** "The pen had power, but fate did not. This ring is now just a joke."
*   **Visual Hook (End):** The Gold Ring reflects a Red Neon light.

### Scene 2: The Reality
*   **Visual Hook (Start):** Match Cut: The Red Neon reflection becomes a Red Bike Taillight.
*   **Audio Bridge (L-Cut):** The rain from Scene 1 continues seamlessly.
*   **Veo 3 Prompt:**
    ```text
    [SUBJECT]: Mid Shot of Same Man (wearing raincoat) + Revving Zomato Bike + Rain falling heavily :: [ENVIRONMENT]: Kolkata Traffic Signal :: [LIGHTING]: Red Taillights reflecting on wet face :: [CAMERA]: Dolly Track alongside bike :: [POST]: Bleach Bypass (Cold) --style cinematic
    ```
*   **Dialogue (Bengali):** "মাস্টার অফ কমার্স। অথচ হিসেব মিলাচ্ছি অন্যের ভাতের প্লেটের। ডিগ্রিটা কি বাইকের পেট্রোল হবে?"
*   **Translation:** "Master of Commerce. Yet I am tallying plates of rice for others. Will the degree become petrol for the bike?"
*   **Visual Hook (End):** The bike creates a splash of water that covers the lens.

### Scene 3: The Stagnation
*   **Visual Hook (Start):** The water clearing reveals a blurry crowd (Focus Pull).
*   **Audio Bridge (J-Cut):** The sound of traffic fades into a collective hum/chant of a crowd.
*   **Veo 3 Prompt:**
    ```text
    [SUBJECT]: Wide Shot of Female Candidate holding 2016 Admit Card + Surrounded by crowd :: [ENVIRONMENT]: Dharna Manch + Dusty posters :: [LIGHTING]: Harsh Mid-day Sun :: [CAMERA]: Split Diopter (Card vs Face) :: [POST]: Documentary Grit --style cinematic
    ```
*   **Dialogue (Bengali):** "হাজার দিন কেটে গেল। বিচার চাই না, শুধু জানতে চাই—আমরা কি বেঁচে আছি, নাকি খাতায়-কলমে মৃত?"
*   **Translation:** "A thousand days have passed. I don't want justice, I just want to know—are we alive, or dead on paper?"
